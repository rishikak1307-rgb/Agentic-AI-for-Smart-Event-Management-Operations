Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about EventOps AI and its event management features.

EventOps AI is an agent-powered event management platform designed to coordinate registrations, venues, speakers, sponsorships and incidents from a central system.
The proposed system includes Registration, Venue, Speaker, Sponsorship and Incident agents. The Event Intelligence Engine combines their information to support event-wide decisions.
The attendee dashboard is designed to support event browsing, registration and self-check-in. These features must be connected to the application's backend to work with real event data.
Select Technical Support or Incident Management in the enquiry form and describe the issue, its location and its urgency. For urgent real-world incidents, contact the on-site event response team directly.
The form sends a POST request to /contact. Your Flask backend must implement message handling, validation and storage or email delivery. The HTML page alone does not send an email.
