ABOUT EVENTOPS AI

One intelligent platform for every event operation.

Organising conferences, hackathons, exhibitions and corporate summits involves registrations, venues, speakers, sponsorships and unexpected incidents. Managing these activities separately can create delays, communication gaps and operational challenges.

EventOps AI brings these activities together through a centralised platform. Its specialised agents support individual tasks, while the Event Intelligence Engine analyses operational information and provides recommendations to event organisers.

The goal is to help organisers make informed decisions, reduce repetitive work and provide attendees with a smoother event experience.
