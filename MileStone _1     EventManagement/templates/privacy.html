About this privacy policy

This page is a starter privacy policy for the EventOps AI project. It describes intended privacy practices, not verified technical guarantees. 01 Overview

EventOps AI is an agent-powered event management platform intended for conferences, hackathons, exhibitions and corporate summits. It supports event registration, venue management, speaker coordination, sponsorship tracking and incident handling.

This policy applies to information processed through the EventOps AI website and its attendee and administrator dashboards. Access to information should be limited according to the user's role and the purpose for which it is needed.

02 Information we may collect

Depending on the features enabled, the application may process:

Data category Examples Purpose
Account details Name, email, account ID Login and account management
Registration data Event choice, ticket, registration status Organising attendance
Check-in data Check-in status and recorded time Attendance management
Event operations Session, venue, incident details Operational coordination
Technical data Error logs and system activity Troubleshooting and security

Only collect data that is necessary for the feature being used. Do not collect sensitive information without a clearly defined need, appropriate safeguards and a valid legal basis.

03 How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage attendee accounts and registrations.
  • Generate tickets and record event check-ins.
  • Coordinate venues, speakers, schedules and sponsor deliverables.
  • Send event updates and operational notifications.
  • Identify capacity issues and operational bottlenecks.
  • Investigate incidents, prevent misuse and improve reliability.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any required notice or consent.

04 AI agents and automated processing

EventOps AI is designed around five specialised agents: Registration, Venue, Speaker, Sponsorship and Incident. The Event Intelligence Engine combines operational information to estimate event health, identify possible bottlenecks and suggest actions.

  • AI recommendations may depend on the accuracy of available data.
  • Organisers should review important recommendations before acting.
  • Access to agent results should be restricted to authorised users.
  • Do not send personal data to external AI services unless the integration, purpose and applicable safeguards have been reviewed.

If AI is used to make decisions that significantly affect individuals, explain that processing clearly and provide appropriate human review and other safeguards where required.

05 Sharing information

Personal information should only be shared when needed to operate the service, fulfil a disclosed purpose, comply with legal obligations or address a legitimate security concern.

  • Event organisers: may access registration and attendance details needed to manage their events.
  • Service providers: may process information for hosting, email delivery or other enabled integrations.
  • Legal requirements: information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

EventOps AI should not claim that it never shares information unless the actual application and its service providers support that claim.

06 Data security

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be implemented to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

  • Use secure password storage and appropriate authentication.
  • Apply role-based access controls to attendee and administrator pages.
  • Use HTTPS in deployed environments.
  • Validate form input and protect against common web vulnerabilities.
  • Restrict database access and protect backups and application secrets.
  • Establish a process for investigating and responding to data incidents.

No website or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. These are recommended safeguards and must be implemented in the actual application.

07 Data retention and deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, contractual requirements, applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

The project administrator should define retention periods for accounts, registrations, check-in records, incidents, system logs and backups. Where appropriate, information should be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required.

08 Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, users may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or other action concerning their personal information.

  • Keep account details accurate and up to date.
  • Contact the application administrator with a privacy request.
  • Ask how information is used and how long it is retained.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and withdrawal is available under applicable law.

Requests may require identity verification. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

09 Cookies and local storage

The application may use cookies or browser storage for functions such as maintaining a login session, remembering a theme preference or improving usability. The actual technologies depend on the implementation.

Authentication cookies and similar technologies should be configured securely. If analytics or third-party tracking is introduced, disclose it here and obtain consent where required.

10 Student and minor privacy

Events may involve students or people under the age of 18. Organisers should collect only necessary information and apply age-appropriate safeguards, notices and consent processes where required by applicable law.

Do not publish student registration details, contact information or attendance records publicly. Restrict access to authorised personnel and follow the relevant institution's policies.

11 Changes to this policy

This policy may need updating when EventOps AI introduces new features, changes data providers or changes how information is processed. The published policy should show its effective date and explain significant changes.
